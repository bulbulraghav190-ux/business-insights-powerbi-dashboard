# Business Insights | Interactive Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze retail business performance across sales, profitability, and customer behavior — with full interactive slicer-based filtering.

> 🛠️ This dashboard was designed and built by **me** as a personal/portfolio project.

---

## 📊 Overview

The report contains **4 pages**, each focused on a different lens of the business:

### 1. Executive Dashboard
A high-level summary view with key KPIs and trends:
- **KPIs:** Total Revenue (39.34M), Total Profit (7.87M), Total Orders (100K), Total Units (300K), Profit Margin (20.02%), Average Order Value (393.60)
- **Monthly Revenue Trend** – seasonal revenue pattern across the year
- **Revenue by Category** – category-wise revenue breakdown (Fruits, Grocery, Snacks, Beverages, Home Care, Vegetables, Personal Care, Dairy)
- **Profit by Category** – profitability comparison across categories
- **Sales by Channel** – Omnichannel vs Online vs Offline performance

### 2. Sales Performance
A deeper look into sales and profit drivers:
- **Profit by Channel** – channel-wise profit comparison
- **Sales by Store Format** – Hyper, Super, and Express store formats
- **Top 10 Brands** – best-performing brands by revenue (ITC, HUL, Britannia, Nestle, Amul, Tata, PepsiCo, Parle, etc.)
- **City Performance** – revenue by city across Kolkata, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru

### 3. Customer Analysis
Customer-centric metrics and demographics:
- **Revenue by Gender** – donut chart split across F, M, O, and unspecified
- **Revenue by Payment Method** – UPI, Card, Wallet, and Cash usage share
- **Customer Age Analysis** – revenue distribution across customer age groups (20–65+)

### 4. Interactive Analysis
A fully slicer-driven control panel enabling dynamic filtering across all report pages:
- **Year** slicer
- **Category** slicer (Beverages, Fruits, Home Care, Snacks, Dairy, Grocery, Personal Care, Vegetables)
- **Channel** slicer (Offline, Omnichannel, Online)
- **City** slicer (Ahmedabad, Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata)
- **Invoice Date** range slicer
- **Customer Gender** slicer (F, M, O, nan)

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** – data modeling, DAX measures, and report design
- **DAX** – for KPI calculations (Revenue, Profit, Margin, AOV, etc.)
- **Data Visualization** – bar charts, donut charts, line charts, and slicers for interactivity

---

## 🎯 Key Insights
- Revenue is fairly evenly distributed across product categories, with **Fruits** leading slightly.
- **Omnichannel** is the strongest performer across both sales and profit.
- Payment methods are almost evenly split across **UPI, Card, Wallet, and Cash**.
- Revenue by gender is nearly balanced between Male and Female customers.
- Top cities and top brands show tight, competitive performance with no single outlier dominating.
